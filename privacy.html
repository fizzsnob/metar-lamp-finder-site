Privacy Policy

METAR Lamp Finder · Effective July 19, 2026

METAR Lamp Finder does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

The app browses your local Wi-Fi network using Apple's Bonjour (mDNS) service discovery to find METAR lamps and other web servers that advertise themselves. This discovery happens entirely on your device and on your local network. Nothing the app sees — device names, addresses, or anything else — ever leaves your device. The app contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party services, and it creates no account.

The app requests the iOS Local Network permission solely to perform this discovery. Tapping a discovered device opens its web page in Safari; from that point the connection is between Safari and the device on your own network.

Questions? Contact bell@mac.com.
