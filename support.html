METAR Lamp Finder

METAR Lamp Finder discovers METAR lamps on your local Wi-Fi network and opens the one you tap in Safari.

No lamps appear?

  • Make sure Local Network access is allowed: Settings ▸ METAR Lamps ▸ Local Network.
  • Your iPhone/iPad and the lamps must be on the same Wi-Fi network (not a guest network, not cellular).
  • The app scans continuously — lamps appear on their own as they come online. Pull down on the list or tap the arrow to force a fresh sweep.
  • To list every web server on the network (printers, bridges, and more), turn on Show all web servers in the app's Settings.

What the labels mean

  • Each lamp usually advertises two pages: Setup & control and METAR-only display. A Settings toggle can collapse this to one entry per lamp.
  • A red SW Update Available pill means the lamp reports a waiting firmware update — open the lamp and use its own update control.
